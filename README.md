## 📖 Academic Theory Modules

### 👶 1. Pediatric Health Nursing-I (02 CH)
> Child health concepts, growth and development milestones, pediatric health assessment, and management of systemic childhood disorders.
- 📖 [Lecture Notes & Growth Milestones](./pediatric/unit1.md)
- 📝 [Pediatric Practice Quiz](https://forms.google.com)

---

### 🏘️ 2. Community Health Nursing-I (02 CH)
> Principles of epidemiology, primary health care strategies, health promotion, and community assessment frameworks.
- 📖 [Epidemiology & Primary Care Notes](./chn/unit1.md)
- 📝 [CHN Practice Quiz](https://forms.google.com)

---

### 🤰 3. Reproductive Health (02 CH)
> Maternal physiology, antenatal care, labor process, postnatal maternal care, and family planning concepts.
- 📖 [Antenatal Care & Maternal Health Notes](./reproductive/unit1.md)

---

### 🎓 4. Teaching/Learning: Principles/Practices (03 CH)
> Learning theories, instructional methods, curriculum principles, and patient/client health education design.
- 📖 [Educational Theories & Patient Education Notes](./teaching-learning/notes.md)

---

### ⚖️ 5. Nursing Ethics (01 CH)
> Ethical theories, bioethics principles, legal responsibilities, patient advocacy, and professional code of ethics.
- 📖 [Code of Ethics & Decision Making Notes](./ethics/notes.md)

---

### ✍️ 6. English Proficiency Course-3 (EPC 3) (02 CH)
> Advanced academic writing, medical terminology, critical reading, and professional healthcare documentation.
- 📖 [Academic Writing & Communication Skills Notes](./english/notes.md)

---

### 🌟 7. PERLS 05 (01 CH)
> Professionalism, ethics integration, research application, and leadership development in nursing.
- 📖 [Professionalism & Leadership Notes](./perls/notes.md)
